About this website

This website belongs to the software that produces and publishes The Quiet Archive's videos. The software is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It prepares each video and, after the owner has reviewed and approved it, uploads it to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API. It is not a public service, has no user accounts, and does not collect data from visitors. Please read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

How the videos are made

  • Scripts are written with an AI language model and reviewed by the channel owner before anything is published.
  • Narration is a synthetic voice.
  • Pictures are created with an AI image generator.
  • Every video is approved by the owner before it is uploaded, and is marked on YouTube as containing altered or synthetic content.
  • The videos are for relaxation and storytelling, not for study or research.
