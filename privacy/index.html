Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

The Quiet Archive ("we", "the app") is a private tool operated by one person to produce calm, sleep-friendly history videos and upload them to its own YouTube channel, "The Quiet Archive". This policy explains what information the app handles.

Who uses the app

Only the owner of the channel. The app is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and no other person signs in to it.

Google and YouTube data

When the owner signs in with Google, the app receives an authorization token that lets it upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel (permission: youtube.upload). The app uses this permission only to upload videos that the owner has reviewed and approved, and to set their details: title, description, thumbnail, visibility and the "altered or synthetic content" disclosure.

The app does not read the owner's watch history, comments, analytics, subscribers or private videos, and it does not access any other person's Google or YouTube data.

The app's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where information is stored

The authorization token, the videos and the app's records are stored on the owner's own private server. They are not sold, shared, or sent to any third party except YouTube, as needed to upload.

Services used to make the videos

The scripts are written by an AI language model, and the pictures are created by an AI image generator. Only the text of the story and short descriptions of the pictures are sent to those services. No Google user data and no information about viewers is sent to them.

Visitors to this website

This website has no accounts, forms, advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. Like most web servers, ours may keep standard technical logs (IP address, time and page requested) for security and troubleshooting. We do not use them to identify visitors and do not share them.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can remove the app's access to YouTube at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that the app can no longer upload. The owner can also delete the stored token from the server. To ask about any information we hold, write to the address below.

Children

The app and this website are not directed at children and do not knowingly collect information from them.
